# Obsiman

**Capture · Structure · Connect · Explore**

Obsiman is a software engineering initiative focused on extending Obsidian and Markdown-based knowledge environments through plugins, themes, visualization systems and configurable workspaces.

Our work explores how independently maintained information can become structured, interconnected and operationally useful while preserving ownership of the underlying content.

## Purpose & Motivation

Obsiman exists to explore the possibilities of knowledge management beyond traditional note-taking.

Personal and organizational knowledge often becomes fragmented across documents, applications and information systems, making relationships difficult to discover and maintain.

The objective is to develop tools that improve how people capture, organize, visualize and interact with information without introducing unnecessary dependencies or compromising data portability.

## Areas of Focus

- **Knowledge Management:** Structuring and connecting personal and organizational information.
- **Obsidian Extensions:** Plugins that extend the capabilities of Obsidian-based workspaces.
- **Metadata-Driven Systems:** Using properties and relationships to enable configurable information experiences.
- **Information Visualization:** Graphs, tables, dashboards and alternative representations of knowledge.
- **Content Presentation:** Configurable layouts, themes and document experiences.
- **Synchronization:** Exploring reliable information access across devices and storage environments.

## Key Initiative Examples

The following initiatives illustrate selected applications of our work:

- **Enterprise Hub:** A configurable knowledge workspace combining vault-native information with structured views and reusable workspace capabilities.
- **[Enterprise Theme](https://github.com/obsiman/enterprise-theme):** A typography-led visual system for configurable Obsidian experiences.
- **Base Views:** Exploration of alternative structured information views.
- **Graph:** Exploration of knowledge relationships and graph-based information discovery.
- **Content Layouts:** Configurable approaches to document and information presentation.
- **OneDrive Sync:** Exploration of synchronization between Obsidian vaults and external storage.

## Approach

Information is treated as an independently owned resource that should remain accessible beyond any individual application or presentation layer.

Capabilities are developed as focused extensions and reusable systems, allowing different knowledge environments to adopt the functionality appropriate to their needs.

Where possible, presentation, operations and derived information are separated from canonical content.

## Engineering Principles

- **Information Ownership:** Users should retain control over their underlying information.
- **Portability:** Content should remain accessible through independent tools and open formats.
- **Extensibility:** Capabilities should be independently configurable and reusable.
- **Separation of Responsibilities:** Content, operations and presentation should remain distinguishable.
- **Progressive Enhancement:** Additional functionality should complement existing knowledge environments.
- **Maintainability:** Prefer explicit and understandable architectures over unnecessary complexity.
